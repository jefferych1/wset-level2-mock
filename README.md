# WSET Level 2 Wines – Unofficial Mock Exam

A free practice exam for the WSET Level 2 Award in Wines.

- Full mock: 50 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, drawn at random from a bank of 370
- Same weighting as the real paper: 5 / 4 / 19 / 12 / 6 / 4 across the six learning outcomes
- Graded like the real exam: Pass 55%, Merit 70%, Distinction 85%
- Practice papers for each learning outcome
- Review of every question you got wrong, with the correct answer and an explanation

**Play it:** https://jefferych1.github.io/wset-level2-mock/

Unofficial study aid. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wine & Spirit Education Trust (WSET). Questions are original, written against the published Level 2 Award in Wines specification.
